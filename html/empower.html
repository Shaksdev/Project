Empowerment Programme

The OPay Empowerment Programme (OPEP) equips individuals with practical skills, financial support, and access to opportunities to reduce poverty and unemployment in Nigeria.

Program Summary

Vision:

To create a self-reliant society by empowering individuals with skills, resources, and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods and national development.

Mission:

To empower underserved individuals and communities in Nigeria with the tools, knowledge, and financial support needed for economic independence and sustainable growth.

Target Beneficiaries

Marginalized Youth, women, and and girls, especially those in underserved communities.

Aims and Objectives

  1. To Enhance Skill Development.
  2. To Promote Financial Inclusion.
  3. Support Job Creation.
